# Plastic Surgery & Aesthetic Medicine Marketing

Plastic surgery and aesthetic medicine is a high-consideration, high-value vertical where the typical patient decision cycle runs 3–6 months and average procedure values range from $8,000 to $25,000. These economics make SEO and content the highest-ROI acquisition channel for practices with a compounding system — and make single-channel paid-only approaches plateau quickly.

## What makes this vertical different

### Compliance constraints

- **Meta/Instagram**: restricts before/after imagery in paid ads; prohibits targeting based on body image insecurities.
- **HIPAA**: governs how patient data can be used in retargeting and remarketing.
- **FTC**: requires disclosure for paid patient testimonials and endorsements.
- **Google healthcare advertising**: certain cosmetic procedure keywords require LegitScript certification.

Agencies without vertical experience routinely burn budget on disapproved ads or expose practices to compliance risk.

### Patient decision cycle

Prospective patients for elective aesthetic procedures typically research for 3–6 months across multiple sessions before booking a consultation. Single-touch paid search captures bottom-of-funnel only; the full patient acquisition system requires content that nurtures across the research cycle.
