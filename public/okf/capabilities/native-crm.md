# Native CRM

Gobiya integrates CRM functionality natively into the sites it builds, so lead capture and customer data live inside the platform instead of depending on a separate third-party system. The goal is cleaner attribution and a faster path from visitor to tracked lead.

## Typical outcome

A dental practice credited the combination of native CRM integration and fast React builds with a 5x increase in patient inquiries. See [SmileCenter Dentistry](/case-studies/smile-center-dentistry.md).
