# SEO Traffic Recovery

SEO traffic recovery is the process of diagnosing exactly what caused an organic traffic drop and executing the specific fix that restores rankings. The most common mistake after a traffic loss is making changes before identifying the cause. Gobiya runs every recovery forensic-read first, targeted-fix second — which is what compresses recovery from quarters to weeks.

## What it covers

- **Update identification**: matching the timing and pattern of a traffic drop to the specific Google algorithm update responsible (core update, helpful content, spam, link spam, etc.).
- **Penalty forensics**: distinguishing between algorithmic suppression and a manual action in Search Console, and diagnosing the root cause of each.
- **Technical failure diagnosis**: crawl blocks, noindex errors, redirect chains, canonical conflicts — technical causes that produce traffic drops identical to algorithmic ones.
- **Competitive shift analysis**: identifying when a traffic decline reflects a market change (competitor domain authority growth, SERP feature displacement) rather than a penalization event.
- **Targeted fix execution**: once the cause is mapped, shipping the precise correction rather than a broad SEO refresh.
- **Recovery monitoring**: Search Console diagnostics and rank tracking through the fix cycle and subsequent update windows.
