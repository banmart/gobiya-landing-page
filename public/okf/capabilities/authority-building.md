# Authority Building

Authority building strengthens the signals that tell both search engines and AI systems what a brand is trusted to know. It is the repair work that supports a recovery and the foundation that keeps a site resilient through future updates.

## What it covers

- Repairing authority signals damaged by an algorithm hit (for example, recovering from content-decay patterns across many pages).
- Building the topical and entity signals that make a brand a confident, retrievable answer.

## Why it matters

AI search rewards entity association — how confidently a model connects a brand to a topic based on signals across the whole web. Authority work is what turns a recovered site into one that AI systems will cite.
