# AI & LLMs for Business

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring a website's content and entity signals so that AI models — including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude — surface the brand when users ask conversational questions. Gobiya offers GEO as a service after AI Overviews began intercepting search traffic in 2024, reducing clicks to sites that ranked well in traditional search but were invisible in AI-generated answers.

The difference between ranking in Google and appearing in an AI answer is largely about content structure: AI models quote content that directly answers a question in plain language, not content written as a service pitch. Gobiya restructures existing pages and writes new content specifically to be cited, not just indexed.

## What it covers

- Generative engine optimization (GEO): structuring content so AI assistants can extract and cite it.
- AI content systems that compound over time.
- AI-layer monitoring and live signal adaptation.

## Typical outcome

For a local service brand, Gobiya deployed GEO-optimized content across 12 service areas; the brand now appears in AI search results ahead of the first organic click. See [SmileCenter Dentistry](/case-studies/smile-center-dentistry.md).
