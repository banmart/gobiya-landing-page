# SEO & Discoverability

This is GOBIYA's core service: recovering organic search visibility lost to Google algorithm updates and keeping it. The work starts with a forensic read of a site's signal history — traffic curves, the update timeline, and content patterns — to pinpoint the cause before anything is changed.

## What it covers

- Update forensics: matching a traffic drop to the specific Google update that caused it.
- SERP history and pattern matching across the site's content.
- Corrective fixes: content restructuring, authority repair, and technical corrections, deployed in one coordinated push rather than a long roadmap.

## Typical outcome

In one documented engagement, an e-commerce site that lost 74% of organic traffic after the Helpful Content Update was diagnosed (a thin-content pattern), restructured, and recovered within six weeks. See [E-commerce HCU recovery](/case-studies/ecommerce-hcu-recovery.md).
