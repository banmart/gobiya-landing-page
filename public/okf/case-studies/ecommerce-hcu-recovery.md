# E-commerce — Helpful Content recovery

**Outcome:** organic rankings restored within six weeks, tracked over 36 months.

## The hit

An e-commerce site lost roughly 74% of its organic traffic following Google's Helpful Content Update.

## The fix

GOBIYA's forensic read identified the cause as a thin-content cluster. The team restructured the affected content and repaired authority signals across the site.

## The curve

Rankings recovered within six weeks of the fix, and the recovery held across the months that followed.
