# Gobiya

Gobiya is an SEO and web development agency in Los Angeles, California. Steve Martin started the practice in 2010 and incorporated it as Gobiya LLC in 2012. The agency specializes in recovering organic search traffic lost to Google algorithm updates, technical SEO, local SEO, Google Ads management, custom React and Next.js web development, and content optimized to appear in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Gobiya is BBB A+ rated. Steve Martin is bilingual in English and Spanish and has 25+ years of experience in web development and digital marketing. The agency serves clients in home services, dental, real estate, B2B SaaS, e-commerce, and professional services across Los Angeles County and the U.S.

## About Steve Martin

Steve Martin founded Gobiya in 2010 after a decade in web development. He began his career in 1996 at AT&T WorldNet in Burbank providing web technology support, then built a video content distribution platform for Sony Music artists at Webcastr.com in West Hollywood from 2000 to 2005. He has worked in SEO since Google's early years and has tracked every major algorithm update since Panda (2011) inside active client accounts — Penguin, Hummingbird, Medic, BERT, the Helpful Content Update, and the 2024 core updates.

He holds a Certificate in Website Design from Glendale Career College (1996) and was a certified Google Partner for Google Ads and Analytics from 2015 to 2019. He is bilingual in English and Spanish. Technical stack: React, Next.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, JavaScript, WordPress.
