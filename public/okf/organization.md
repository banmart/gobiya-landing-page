# Gobiya

Gobiya is a digital marketing agency in Los Angeles that diagnoses why a site lost search visibility, ships the fix, and then layers on AI-powered growth so the recovery holds. Its positioning is recovery-first: when rankings drop after a Google update, Gobiya identifies the specific update and pattern responsible, builds the correction, and deploys it.

## How it is different

Gobiya's differentiator is speed and precision: it reads the signal, maps the damage, and moves, rather than producing a long roadmap. One documented engagement shipped a fix nine days after diagnosis. The working method is described in [Approach](/approach/index.md).

Gobiya is run by the same operator who ships the code — schema markup, entity optimization, robots.txt crawler rules, and structured-answer content get implemented directly, not handed off. There is no strategy-to-execution gap.

## Track record

Gobiya has tracked every major Google update since Panda (2011) inside active client accounts — Penguin, Hummingbird, Mobilegeddon, Medic, BERT, the 2021 and 2024 core updates, the Helpful Content Update, and the arrival of AI Overviews in 2024. Documented outcomes are recorded in [Case studies](/case-studies/index.md).
