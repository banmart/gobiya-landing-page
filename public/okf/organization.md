# GOBIYA

GOBIYA is a digital marketing agency in Los Angeles that diagnoses why a site lost search visibility, ships the fix, and then layers on AI-powered growth so the recovery holds. Its positioning is recovery-first: when rankings drop after a Google update, GOBIYA identifies the specific update and pattern responsible, builds the correction, and deploys it.

## What GOBIYA does

- **SEO algorithm & penalty recovery** — forensic diagnosis of update-driven traffic loss and the corrective fix. See [SEO & Discoverability](/capabilities/seo-discoverability.md).
- **Generative engine optimization (GEO)** — content architecture built so the brand surfaces in AI search results. See [AI & LLMs for Business](/capabilities/ai-llms-business.md).
- **Authority building** — strengthening the off-site and on-site signals that establish topical authority. See [Authority Building](/capabilities/authority-building.md).
- **Web development** — fast, modern React builds engineered to rank and convert. See [Web Development](/capabilities/web-development.md).
- **Native CRM** — lead capture and customer data integrated directly into the build. See [Native CRM](/capabilities/native-crm.md).
- **Blockchain & Web3** — Web3 and blockchain development. See [Blockchain & Web3](/capabilities/blockchain-web3-development.md).

A full list lives in [Capabilities](/capabilities/index.md).

## How it is different

GOBIYA's claim is speed and precision: it reads the signal, maps the damage, and moves, rather than producing a long roadmap. One documented engagement shipped a fix nine days after diagnosis. The working method is described in [Approach](/approach/index.md).

## Track record

GOBIYA states it has read every major Google update since Panda (2011) inside client accounts — Penguin, Hummingbird, Mobilegeddon, Medic, BERT, the 2021 and 2024 core updates, the Helpful Content Update, and the arrival of AI Overviews in 2024. Documented outcomes are recorded in [Case studies](/case-studies/index.md).
