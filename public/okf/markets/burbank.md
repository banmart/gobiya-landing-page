# Burbank — Local SEO Market

Gobiya has served clients in the Burbank, California market since 2012. Burbank operates as a distinct commercial sub-market within Greater Los Angeles, shaped by the concentration of entertainment and media industry tenants (Warner Bros., Disney, NBC, Nickelodeon) and the dense professional, medical, and home services sectors along San Fernando Boulevard.

## ZIP codes served

## What makes this market distinct

- Entertainment and media industry concentration creates B2B search patterns not found in adjacent San Fernando Valley markets.
- Dental, medical, and professional service practices compete across Burbank, Glendale, Toluca Lake, Studio City, and North Hollywood simultaneously — requiring multi-geography GBP service area configuration.
- Home services competitors from the broader Valley frequently have stronger citation profiles than Burbank-specific operators, making NAP consistency especially high-impact.

## Key local SEO signals for this market

- **GBP categories**: category selection is competitive in medical and home services — wrong primary category is the most common ranking gap.
- **Citation sources**: Google, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and vertical directories (Healthgrades, Houzz, Angi) carry the most weight.
- **Review velocity**: review count and recency are direct Map Pack ranking signals; Burbank practices with 18+ months of review inactivity consistently underperform newer entrants.
- **LocalBusiness schema**: implementation is low across Burbank SMBs — a technical advantage for practices that do it correctly.
